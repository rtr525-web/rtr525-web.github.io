WPR #01 · May 1981Lake Rose

The 1981 Sinkhole That Became Lake Rose

In May 1981, a massive karst sinkhole opened on West Fairbanks Avenue, swallowing a three-bedroom house, five Porsches, a camper, and the deep end of the municipal Olympic pool.

WPR #02 · 1881Historic District

Hannibal Square — The West Side That Built Winter Park

Founded in 1881 for the Black families, carpenters, masons, and citrus workers who built Winter Park's railroads and streets — and whose votes in 1887 secured the town charter.

WPR #03 · 1950Lake Osceola

Albin Polasek — The Sculptor Who Worked With One Hand

After suffering a stroke at age 70 upon retiring to Winter Park, Czech-American sculptor Albin Polasek carved and modeled over a dozen monumental works using only his right hand.

WPR #04 · 1933 / 2000Architecture

Casa Feliz — The 750-Ton House They Hauled Across Town

Designed by James Gamble Rogers II in 1933 from century-old Andalusian brick, the Barbour estate was saved from demolition in 2000 by rolling all 750 tons down Interlachen Avenue.

WPR #05 · 1930sChain of Lakes

Genius Drive & The Peacocks of Wind Song

How Charles Hosmer Morse's granddaughter Jeannette Genius McKean turned a private lakeside road between Lake Mizell and Lake Virginia into a sanctuary for art and peafowl.

YouTube › Instagram ›
WPR #06 · 1932Rollins Campus

Annie Russell Theatre — Florida's Historic Stage

Built in 1932 on the Rollins College campus in Spanish Colonial Revival style for stage actress Annie Russell — the longest continuously operating theater in Florida.

YouTube › Instagram ›
WPR #07 · 1938Lake Maitland

Kraft Azalea Garden — The Exedra on Lake Maitland

Opened in 1938 on a shaded 5.2-acre peninsula jutting into Lake Maitland, anchored by giant bald cypresses and the neoclassical Exedra monument.

WPR #08 · 1940Botanical

Theodore Mead's 5,000 Orchids — Mead Botanical Garden

Naturalist Theodore L. Mead spent five decades hybridizing orchids and caladiums in Central Florida before his friends established the 47-acre Mead Botanical Garden in 1940.

WPR #09 · 1938Logging Canals

The Scenic Boat Tour — Running the Canals Since 1938

Captain Walt Meloon launched the Scenic Boat Tour in 1938, guiding boats through the narrow 19th-century timber logging canals connecting Lakes Osceola, Virginia, and Maitland.

WPR #10 · 1942Park Avenue

The Morse Museum's Tiffany Treasure

When Louis Comfort Tiffany's Long Island estate Laurelton Hall burned in 1957, Hugh and Jeannette McKean rescued its surviving leaded glass and 1893 chapel for Winter Park.
