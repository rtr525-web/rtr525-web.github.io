Our story

From one channel to a network

It started with one creator in Minneola, Florida, teaching practical things clearly — sprinkler repair, welding, golf, local news — in a consistent "blueprint" style.

Channel by channel the network grew. In October 2026 it was organized into one registry and seven desks, with a simple rule: nothing publishes until its facts are verified. By November 2026 it had passed 80 channels and Pages.

  • No paid placement, everIn any list, ranking, review or video.
  • Verified facts or cut itEvery piece passes a quality check first.
  • Clear labelsEducation is labeled education; folklore is labeled folklore.
  • Respect for creatorsProperly licensed media only.
