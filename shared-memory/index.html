Shared Memory Hub

The crew's shared rules — one reference for Muse, Claude, and Gemini. Created Wednesday, November 4, 2026, at Robert's order. Updated every time a conversation sets or changes a standing rule; every change is dated in the Change Log at the bottom.

This hub holds only the rules everyone works under: shared ethics, FDC policy (public rules only), Florida law, state/federal law reference points, and Facebook/Meta policy. It is a public page — empire rules and public law/policy only. No personal, financial, family, health, employer-facility, or case details ever go in here.

A. Shared Ethics

The ethics screen (before anything posts — every piece, every time). Every piece passes two checks before it can publish: (1) the platform's current community guidelines, and (2) the FDC off-duty conduct pre-screen (Section B). A failed screen stages the piece and reports it the same day — it never posts, and it is never quietly fixed and posted.

Education only. Everything the empire publishes is education. No selling, no prices, no retailer links, no "for sale" or "where to buy" language — on any lane. The single exception is the Ko-fi tip jar. Brand disclaimers ride every post where they apply: finance lanes say "General info, not financial advice"; health lanes say "General health info"; legal lanes end "Education only, not legal advice"; cigar lanes say "Education only. Nothing for sale."

No static posts. Every post is a narrated video with word-for-word captions — no flat text or image posts anywhere in the empire.

Replace, then archive — never delete. A replacement goes live first; only then is the old version archived (YouTube Private, Facebook archive). Old posts are not deleted. (Two exceptions Robert ordered himself: retiring duplicate brand channels, and re-posting corrected pieces where he directed delete-and-repost.)

Quotes. Every quote must be real, from a real author, properly attributed, and verified against a reliable source before use. Never invented, altered, or misattributed. No Steve Jobs quotes, ever.

Sources and authors on every claim. Every study question, study guide, and researched claim carries its source and author(s) — a rule/statute citation for policy material, a named author and work for research material. Crew-authored practice or judgment with no external source is labeled as such, never presented as sourced fact.

Source Interest Rule. Material pulled from another account is used only if the subject is genuinely interesting — a real story or hook, visual potential, a notable name or fact, or a local angle. A dull or administrative source post is skipped with the reason logged, never mechanically converted.

Every tip carries its reason. Every tip published explains why it works and the problem it solves — never the tip alone.

Privacy rule (public pages). Public pages carry empire content only. Never publish personal, financial, family, health, or employer details, home addresses, or case specifics. If in doubt, leave it out.

Approvals discipline. Empire posts that are gate-certified are pre-approved by default under Robert's standing order (revocation phrase: "stop auto-approve"); standing holds are never covered by that approval. Everything else needs Robert's specific word: purchases, permanent deletions, email/message sends, Page creation, bio or name changes, and any account change — and anything money, legal, or irreversible. Small, reversible, low-stakes choices (privating or archiving a known duplicate, applying an already-prepped fix, tidying a stale record) are decided and executed by the crew and reported after the fact.

Firearms content. News, discussion, and education only. No links to sites that sell firearms or accessories, no "for sale" or "where to buy" language, no branded or sponsored firearms content, no prices. See Section E for the platform rules behind this.

Pre-publish quality gate. Nothing publishes without passing the quality gate (pacing, word-fit, quality, compliance). A failed gate stages the piece and reports — it never posts.

B. FDC Policy — Public Rules Only

This section is public rule material only — published Florida Administrative Code rules and Florida Statutes. No internal or non-public institutional information appears in any crew content, ever.

Employee code of conduct — FAC 33-208.002 / 33-208.003. The conduct rules apply on AND off duty. The points the crew screens against:

  • No political activity on duty; no use of the position's authority in a partisan election; no state resources or state time for lobbying. (Fla. Stat. §104.31; §110.233(4)(b); §11.062)
  • Never disclose or use non-public institutional information — no staffing, post assignments, schedules, security procedures, or incidents — in any post, letter, or content. (Fla. Stat. §112.313(8))
  • No soliciting funds, petitions, signatures, or literature on agency property or while on duty. (FAC 33-208.002(21))
  • "Conduct Unbecoming" — conduct that discredits the Department is disciplinable even off duty. (FAC 33-208.002(22))
  • Candidacy for office while state-employed is barred, except local office with agency-head and department approval obtained first. (Fla. Stat. §110.233(4)(b))
  • Appearing before the Legislature during agency business hours must be on approved leave or an off day, in civilian clothes, by personal vehicle, recorded in citizen or union-member capacity. (Fla. Stat. §11.061)

Pre-send policy check. Before anything FDC-related is sent or published, the content is checked against FDC policy and procedure, the employee code of conduct (FAC 33-208.002/.003), and facility rules. Content stays public rule/procedure and public news only. Anything that fails does not send, and the failure is reported to Robert instead.

No FDC leadership contact — ever. The crew never reaches out to, or drafts messages to, any administrator in Robert's chain of command. Advocacy targets are elected officials, the Governor's office, the Department of Management Services, the Legislature, and the union — never his own leadership.

Justice Blueprint scope. The channel covers Florida law and law enforcement in general — Florida law, courts, statutes, how the system works. Nothing agency-specific, nothing corrections-specific, nothing about Robert's job. Textbook altitude only; every piece ends "Education only, not legal advice."

Union activity is protected. Union/PBA activity is affirmatively protected under Florida Statutes Chapter 447; §447.501 bars agency interference with or retaliation for it. Advocacy runs through citizen and union channels, on personal time and personal accounts.

Public rule references the crew studies (one-line summaries, public rules only):

  • FAC 33-602.210 (Use of Force): force is authorized only for the purposes in Fla. Stat. §944.35(1)(a); organized force is planned control/escort/relocation, reactionary force is immediate force to stop violence or resistance; force is a last resort, must cease on compliance, and is never punishment.
  • FAC 33-404 (Mental Health): five levels of care — Outpatient, Infirmary, Transitional, Crisis Stabilization, and Corrections Mental Health Treatment Facility Care — with placement by adaptive functioning and treatment need, and an individualized services plan for every inmate receiving services.

C. Florida Law

General information only — not legal advice. These are the consumer-law references the empire's Florida consumer lane actually teaches, each with its statute cite.

  • FDUTPA — Fla. Stat. §501.204(1): unfair or deceptive acts or practices in trade or commerce are unlawful.
  • Private right of action — §501.211(2): a person who has suffered a loss from a §501.204 violation may recover actual damages.
  • Attorney's fees — §501.2105: the prevailing party in FDUTPA litigation may recover reasonable attorney's fees and court costs.
  • Home Solicitation Sales — Fla. Stat. ch. 501.021–501.055: for most sales over $25 made away from the seller's main place of business (for example, signed at the buyer's home), the buyer may cancel in writing by midnight of the third business day after signing; a mailed notice counts from postmark. Under §501.031, the seller must give the buyer written notice of the right to cancel ("Buyer's Right to Cancel"), and if the buyer cancels, the seller may not keep any cash down payment.
  • Gift certificates — §501.95(2)(a): a gift certificate purchased, or credit memo issued, in Florida may not have an expiration date or any post-sale charge or fee (including service, dormancy, account-maintenance, or cash-out fees). Exceptions exist for charitable, employee-incentive, loyalty, and promotional certificates given without a separate charge, and for bank-issued cards redeemable by multiple unaffiliated merchants, which follow federal rules.
  • Motor vehicle repair — §559.920(13) and §559.921(1): part of Florida's Motor Vehicle Repair Act provisions on unlawful acts by repair shops and the enforcement/remedy framework that follows from them.

D. State Law — Federal Reference Points

Reference summaries of the federal provisions the crew's dispute and consumer work actually uses. This section grows only as Robert adds topics — nothing is padded in.

Fair Credit Billing Act (FCBA) basics:

  • 15 U.S.C. §1666(b)(3) and 12 CFR 1026.13(a)(3): certain billing errors — including goods or services not accepted by the consumer or not delivered as agreed — can be disputed with the card issuer.
  • 15 U.S.C. §1666(a): on a proper billing-error notice, the issuer must acknowledge within 30 days and resolve within two billing cycles (no more than 90 days).
  • 15 U.S.C. §1666i and 12 CFR 1026.12(c): a cardholder may assert claims and defenses arising from a transaction against the card issuer, subject to the statute's conditions.
  • 15 U.S.C. §1666a: governs creditor reporting of disputed amounts while a billing-error claim is open.
  • Rigby v. FIA Card Services, 490 F. App'x 230 (11th Cir. 2012): Eleventh Circuit authority the crew's dispute work cites on an issuer's duty to actually investigate the documents provided before deciding a claim.

E. Facebook / Meta Policy

Working summary from the platform guidelines quick-reference compiled October 10, 2026 (sources: Meta Community Standards and YouTube Help Center), with the October 11, 2026 Reels addendum and the October 15, 2026 ethics-gate addendum. The full file lives with the crew's compliance records; this is the summary everyone works from.

Community Standards basics the lanes follow. No hate speech, harassment, threats, graphic violence, nudity, misinformation, spam, or fake engagement. Content must be original — Pages that primarily repost unoriginal content get reduced distribution and demonetization under Meta's 2026 original-content rules; content must be produced by the Page owner or add meaningful new value (fresh analysis, new information).

Page policies. No misleading claims — titles and packaging must match what the content actually delivers. Education framing governs every lane (see the disclaimers in Section A). Posting pace stays human and consistent, never spammy.

Reels rules. No watermarked content from other platforms (a TikTok watermark gets downranked — always upload the clean master, never a re-export). Original or licensed audio only; audio from Facebook's own library is fine. No engagement bait in captions ("share if you agree" reduces reach). Reels under 90 seconds get the most distribution; hook in the first 2 seconds.

Firearms on Meta. Facebook restricts firearms sales — no buy/sell posts, no private-sale listings. Discussion, news, and education about firearms are allowed on Pages. The crew goes further (Section A): no sales links or retailer links at all.

Monetization. Facebook Stars requires 500 followers held for 30 days. A payout account must be set up within 6 months of first earning or the earnings are lost. Clean, no-watermark masters are produced per platform.

YouTube platform policy (the empire publishes there too). From the same compiled guidelines: no content intended to sell firearms or accessories and no links to sites that sell them; no firearms manufacturing or conversion instructions; branded or sponsored content featuring weapons or ammunition is prohibited (YouTube policy, September 2026) — never check the paid-promotion box on firearms content. AI-generated or altered content that is realistic must be disclosed at upload. Finance and health content stays general information with its disclaimer — no personalized advice, no guaranteed returns, no medical advice or promised results.

AI-content labeling. Where a platform requires an AI-generated-content label (TikTok offers/requires it on upload; YouTube requires altered-content disclosure for realistic AI content), the label is set — always.
