Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Jayhil Media ("we") runs jayhilmedia.com and the Jayhil Media network of YouTube channels, Facebook Pages and other social accounts. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Contact form: the name, email and message you send us.
  • Newsletter (if you subscribe): your email address, which our email provider stores so we can send the newsletter.
  • Basic site logs: our host (GitHub Pages) may record standard technical data, such as your IP address and browser type, for security.
  • Visitor counter: the homepage footer shows a public visit count from visitor-badge.laobi.icu. Loading it sends that service a standard image request (your IP address and browser type); we receive only the total count, no personal data.

Our YouTube tools

Our "Jayhil YouTube" app uses Google's YouTube API Services only to manage our own channels: uploading our videos and updating their titles, descriptions and playlists. It doesn't access, collect or store data about viewers or other users' accounts. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke access at Google security settings.

How we use it

We use what you send us only to reply to you and to send newsletters you asked for. We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't show paid advertising based on it.

Your choices

Unsubscribe with the link in any newsletter. To have your contact details deleted, use our contact form and we will remove them.
